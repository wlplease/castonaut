Open by design

Castonaut is an independent client. Farcaster access can depend on the network, account, and service provider. We do not claim to restore a banned account.

Follow the build ↗

Connect your Farcaster

Castonaut uses a Neynar managed app signer. Approving it lets Castonaut post, react, recast, and follow on your behalf. You approve access in the Farcaster app and can revoke it in your Farcaster settings. We never ask for your wallet seed phrase.

Your signer stays revocable from your Farcaster account.

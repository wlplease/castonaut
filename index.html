    CHECK THIS IS YOUR ACCOUNTCastonaut is requesting this key. It can only make the social actions above; it cannot move funds. Your wallet will ask you to approve it.
    Only enter this on a site you trust.Page code can read your phrase. Castonaut checks it in this tab, then asks to add the limited key shown above. No funds access. The phrase is cleared after sign-in.
    Checked in this tab · cleared after sign-in
    What can the app key do?
    • Social actions: post and delete your casts, follow or unfollow, like or remove likes, and recast or undo recasts.
    • Cannot: move funds or change account custody.
    • Stored here: a separate app key in this browser’s IndexedDB. The server gets signed messages, never your private key or phrase.
    • Your control: a compromised page or extension could request social actions. Revoke the signer in Farcaster settings; “Forget this browser” only deletes its local key.

ABOUT CASTONAUT

Your Farcaster.
Your own desk.

Castonaut is a friendly, independent place to read the people you follow, keep favorite profiles close, and join the conversation when you’re ready.

A FEED THAT FLOWS

Recent posts, in order.

Catch up with the accounts you follow in one clear chronological feed.

A PLACE THAT FEELS YOURS

Your people on your desk.

Pin profiles you care about on this device and get back to them with one click.

EASY TO JOIN

Read before connecting.

Public feeds are open to read. Connect your account only when you want to post, follow, or react.

BUILT FOR PEOPLE

Open the app and feel at home.

The app team maintains the shared network connection. There’s nothing to host, tune, or install before you can use your feed.

CONNECT YOUR ACCOUNT

Join in with your Farcaster account.

Connect your wallet to post, follow, and react from this browser. It approves a limited key for this app; we never ask for your recovery phrase or permission to move funds.

  1. 01
    Everyday social actionsPost, react, follow, and remove access. No token transfers.
  2. 02
    Your wallet stays yoursCastonaut never receives or stores your wallet recovery phrase.
  3. 03
    Remove access anytimeRevoke from your Farcaster account settings. The key expires after 30 days of inactivity.
Local developer mode. Disposable account only.Never paste your valuable account’s phrase into a web client. This mode exists so the tutorial can be tested locally. The phrase stays in memory, is not saved, and is cleared after authorization.

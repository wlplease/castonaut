    CASTONAUT ACCOUNT

    Connect with your Farcaster phrase.

    Use your Farcaster recovery phrase once to set up a separate, limited Castonaut key in this browser. After setup, this browser remembers the app key. Reading and your private Desk work without signing in.

    ✳CASTONAUT · THIS BROWSERLimited social key for Castonaut.Post, follow, react, and recast · no funds access30-day sliding expiry · revoke anytime in Farcaster settings

    Your recovery phrase is sensitive.Castonaut checks it in this tab and clears the field after use. Only the signed request for your limited app key is relayed to your node. Page code or a browser extension could read anything you enter, so continue only if you trust this client.
    Used in this tab · field cleared after sign-in
    What can the app key do?
    • Social actions: post and delete your casts, follow or unfollow, like or remove likes, and recast or undo recasts.
    • Cannot: move funds or change account custody.
    • Stored here: a separate app key in this browser’s IndexedDB. The server gets signed messages, never your private key or phrase.
    • Your control: a compromised page or extension could request social actions. Revoke the signer in Farcaster settings; “Forget this browser” only deletes its local key.

A QUICK, LIMITED CONNECTION

Join in when you’re ready.

Read without connecting. To take part, your wallet approves a separate app key. Castonaut never asks for your recovery phrase or permission to move funds.

  1. 1SignChoose the wallet that controls your Farcaster account and review its limited authorization.NOW
  2. 2ApproveYour wallet signs a gasless request. Castonaut sends the signed request to Snapchain for confirmation.NEXT
  3. 3DoneYour limited key stays in this browser for up to 30 days. Then you can post, follow, and react.NEXT
Before you connectUse the custody wallet for an existing Farcaster account with storage. You’ll need a browser wallet and the Optimism network. No recovery phrase, transaction, or network fee is requested.
What exactly can this key do?
  • It can post, reply, follow, like, recast, and remove those social actions.
  • It cannot move funds or change account custody.
  • The private key is created as non-extractable browser key material and stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. The server receives signed messages, never the private key.
  • A compromised app page or browser extension could still ask this browser to sign. Revoke the signer in Farcaster account settings; “Forget this browser” only deletes its local key.
